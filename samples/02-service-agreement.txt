MANAGED SERVICES AGREEMENT

This agreement is between Cedar Health Group and Meridian Systems Ltd.
Elena Patel is the account owner at Meridian Systems Ltd.

Meridian Systems Ltd will keep operational audit logs for 12 months and remove
expired logs within seven days. The Security Operations Lead owns this control.

Under the Australian Privacy Principles, Cedar Health Group must respond to
access requests within a reasonable period. Its Privacy Manager, Oliver Grant,
is responsible for reviewing and responding to each request.

Meridian Systems Ltd must report confirmed data breaches to Cedar Health Group
within 48 hours. The incident notice must include affected systems, dates,
containment actions and a named incident coordinator.
