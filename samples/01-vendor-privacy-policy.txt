VENDOR PRIVACY AND RETENTION POLICY

Northstar Analytics Ltd collects customer contact information to provide support.
The Privacy Officer, Maya Chen, owns the customer access request process.
Access requests must be acknowledged within five business days and fulfilled
within 30 days under the GDPR.

Support tickets containing personal information are retained for 24 months
after closure. The Support Operations team is responsible for scheduled deletion.

Northstar Analytics Ltd uses Harbor Cloud Services as its hosting processor.
Harbor Cloud Services must notify the Privacy Officer of a suspected personal
data breach within 24 hours. Sub-processors require prior written approval.

This policy was approved on 15 September 2026.
